# Meio Tooltips

## 2.0.1

- Ready for Meio Sim (coming soon): for warlocks who have it, its Sim DPS line takes the
  place of Leveling BiS's verdict line; other classes keep Leveling BiS's

## 2.0.0

- Now called Meio Tooltips, folder MeioTooltips. The command is /meiotooltips; /tooltipfold
  still works
- Your settings and hidden lines come along when the old MeioTooltipFold folder loads once
  beside the new one: Meio Tooltips waits, takes them over at logout, and says to disable the
  old one. If your addon manager already removed the old folder, you start on the defaults
  (every fold on, nothing hidden)

## 1.4.0

- Every RestedXP guide shows, not only those near your level; many guides take two lines
  instead of widening the tooltip. The guide range setting is gone
- The item's own score sits beside each comparison: Pawn "123.4 +12%", ForeverGear "92 +20",
  GearQuest "#3 72 +12" (and Leveling BiS, once it lets other addons take over its lines)
- A grey bar between Pawn, ForeverGear and GearQuest on the armour line
- Shift shows Pawn's and RestedXP's own lines, as for the other addons

## 1.3.0

- ForeverGear's gain and GearQuest's rank sit on the armour (or damage) line beside Pawn's
  upgrade, one line instead of three; Shift shows their own lines
- Hide any line you never read: hover an item, type /tooltipfold hide and pick its number,
  or /tooltipfold hide <text>. Shift shows them, /tooltipfold unhide brings one back

## 1.2.0

- First release: Pawn's upgrade beside the item's stats, RestedXP's guide lines in one line
  (only guides near your level), Meionomics' price under Sell Price. Shift shows the detail
